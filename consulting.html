Yes. We can advise on market assessment, distribution channels, partner selection and supply planning, supported by our Dubai and Umm Al Quwain offices.

Yes. We support supplier selection, sourcing strategy, logistics planning and inventory planning for food and beverage businesses.

We support ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environment), ISO 45001 (health and safety), ISO 21502 (project management) and ISO 31000 (risk management), along with ISO 22301, ISO 27001, ISO 37000 and ISO 37301. We help with gap assessment, documentation, training and audit readiness.
