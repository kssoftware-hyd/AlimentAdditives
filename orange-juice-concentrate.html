FAQ

Orange Juice Concentrate FAQ

What is the Brix-to-acid ratio in orange concentrate?

The ratio compares sugar (Brix) with acidity. A higher ratio tastes sweeter and milder, and a lower ratio tastes more tart. Tell us the balance your recipe needs.

Can I choose clear or cloudy orange concentrate?

Yes, you can say which you need in your enquiry. Clear concentrate suits drinks that must stay transparent, and cloudy concentrate gives a fuller look and mouthfeel.
