FAQ

Mixed Fruit Concentrate FAQ

What is mixed fruit juice concentrate?

It is a blend of fruit juice concentrates chosen for its natural fruit sugars and mild flavour. Manufacturers use it as a filler to add sweetness and body to a recipe.

How does it replace sugar?

The fruit sugars in the blend provide part of the sweetness that added sugar or syrup would otherwise give. This lets manufacturers reduce the added sugar in drinks and foods.

Can it replace all the added sugar in a recipe?

Usually it replaces part of the added sugar. The right level depends on your recipe, taste and the Brix and sweetness you need, so we recommend testing it in your formulation.

Will it change the taste or colour of my drink?

A mild blend has less impact on flavour, but the fruit content can affect taste and colour to some degree. Tell us your target flavour and colour so we can discuss suitable options.
