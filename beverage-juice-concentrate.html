Juice Concentrates, Fruit Flavours and Clear or Cloudy Options

Juice concentrate suits recipes that need a recognisable fruit taste with a controlled sweetness and acidity. Clear concentrate is chosen when the finished drink must stay transparent. Cloudy concentrate gives a fuller look and mouthfeel. Citrus concentrates are usually compared by their balance of sugar and acidity, while tropical fruits are chosen for aroma and body.

Choose the Right Concentrate for Your Application

The right concentrate depends on your target Brix, taste, colour, pulp and process conditions. Tell us about your application, ask for technical details, and our team will help you source the concentrate and the other ingredients in your formulation.

How do I choose between clear and cloudy juice concentrate?

Clear concentrate suits drinks that must stay transparent, while cloudy concentrate gives a fuller look and mouthfeel. Tell us how your finished drink should look and we will advise on options.

What does Brix mean for juice concentrate?

Brix measures the sugar concentration. Concentrates are specified by Brix, so it is one of the first details we ask for. Compare it with the Brix of your finished drink to plan dilution.
