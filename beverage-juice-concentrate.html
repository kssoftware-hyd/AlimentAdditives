Why juice concentrate

Why Beverage Makers Use Concentrate

Less volume to store and ship

Concentrate has most of the water removed, so it takes less space than single-strength juice.

Flexible for your recipe

Manufacturers dilute and blend concentrate to the Brix and taste their product needs.

One base for many drinks

The same concentrate can go into juices, nectars, squashes and ready-to-drink beverages.

FAQ

Juice Concentrate FAQ

What is juice concentrate?

Juice concentrate is fruit juice with most of the water removed. It is easier to store and transport, and beverage manufacturers dilute and blend it to make juices, nectars and soft drinks.

What does Brix mean?

Brix measures the sugar concentration of juice. Juice concentrates are usually specified by their Brix value, so it is one of the first details we ask for in an enquiry.

What details do I need to share for a quote?

Tell us the fruit and variety, the Brix, whether you need clear or cloudy, the packing and quantity, and the delivery location. Our team will confirm availability and send a commercial offer.

Which juice concentrates can you source?

Common requests include apple, orange, mango, pineapple, grape, pomegranate, guava and lemon. Availability, grade and packing are confirmed on enquiry. If your fruit is not listed, send us your requirement.

Do you supply juice concentrate in the UAE and GCC?

Our focus is the food and beverage industry in the UAE and GCC. Contact us with your requirement and location to discuss supply options.

Can you supply other beverage ingredients with the concentrate?

Yes. We also source acids such as citric acid and malic acid, sweeteners, flavors and extracts, and stabilizers such as pectin and xanthan gum for beverage formulations.
