What is the difference between Alphonso and Totapuri?

Alphonso is sweet and aromatic. Totapuri is milder and slightly tangy, and is often used where more volume is needed. Many recipes use a blend.

Is puree the same as juice concentrate?

Puree keeps the fruit pulp, so it gives a thicker body than a clear juice concentrate. Tell us your application and we will help you choose.
