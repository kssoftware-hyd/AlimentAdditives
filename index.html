Your Food Ingredients Supplier in the UAE and GCC

Aliment Additives is a food ingredient trading company based in Umm Al Quwain, UAE. We help food manufacturers, processors and distributors source food additives, specialty food ingredients and food raw materials, from acids and thickeners to enzymes, sweeteners, dairy ingredients and oils.

Whether you need citric acid, xanthan gum, carrageenan, pectin, whey powder or sucralose, our team supports your requirements with dependable sourcing, competitive commercial offers and responsive service for the UAE and GCC food and beverage industry. FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What food ingredients does Aliment Additives supply?

We source and supply food ingredients, additives and raw materials including acids and acidulants, thickeners and stabilizers, binders and texturizers, minerals, enzymes, functional ingredients, sweeteners, dairy and milk products, oils and fats, flavors and extracts, and specialty ingredients. Browse the full product list for details.

Where is Aliment Additives based?

We are a UAE-based food ingredient trading company located in the Industrial Area, Umm Al Quwain, United Arab Emirates. We also have a branch office in Dubai (AB Plaza 8).

Do you supply citric acid and other food ingredients in Dubai?

Yes. We supply citric acid and a wide range of food ingredients and additives to customers in Dubai and across the UAE, with a branch office in Dubai (AB Plaza 8). Send us the ingredient, grade and quantity for a quote.

Do you supply food manufacturers across the UAE and GCC?

Our focus is the food and beverage industry in the UAE and GCC. We work with food manufacturers, processors and distributors, including customers in Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman. Contact us with your location and requirements to discuss supply options.

Which industries do you support?

We support bakery and confectionery, dairy and milk products, beverages and juices, meat and processed foods, sauces and dressings, snacks and savoury products, and food processing and manufacturing.

How can I request a quote or product details?

Use the enquiry form, message us on WhatsApp at +971 58 558 9794, or email sales@alimentad.com. Tell us the ingredient, grade and quantity you need and our team will respond during business hours, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm.

Can you source an ingredient that is not on your list?

Yes. Send us your requirement and we will help source it. Share the ingredient name, application and expected quantity so we can respond quickly.
