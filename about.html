About us

Connecting Quality Ingredients with Global Opportunities.

Aliment Additives is a UAE-based trading company specializing in the sourcing and supply of food ingredients, specialty additives and raw materials for the food and beverage industry.

We offer a diverse portfolio of quality ingredients designed to support the evolving needs of food manufacturers, processors and distributors. Our product range covers preservatives, acidity regulators, emulsifiers, stabilizers, sweeteners, starches, dairy ingredients, enzymes, antioxidants and other specialty food ingredients.

At Aliment Additives, we believe that successful business relationships are built on quality, reliability and trust. We are committed to understanding our customers’ specific requirements and providing dependable sourcing solutions, competitive commercial offerings and responsive customer service.

Through our supplier network and market knowledge, we aim to deliver consistent supply solutions while maintaining a strong focus on product quality, professional service and long-term business partnerships.
